Stemlight

Free tools for sequencing light shows in xLights. Everything runs in your browser: no accounts, and your audio never leaves your machine.

Stemlight Timing

Drop in a song and get timing tracks: sections, bars and beats, drums, bass, vocal phrases, syllables, notes and lyrics for singing faces.

Stemlight Composer

Lay out a sequence fast: effects snap to your timing tracks, everything has a keyboard shortcut and unlimited undo, and the result opens in xLights as a .xsq.
